What our website is for

Health Made Simple helps people understand medical terms, health conditions, and diagnoses by explaining them in clear, everyday language. We bring researched educational information together in one place so it is easier to learn and know what questions to ask a healthcare professional.

Health Tips

Small everyday habits that can support well-being.

💧

Drink water

Keep water nearby and drink throughout the day. Needs vary by person and activity.

😴

Get enough sleep

A regular sleep schedule can help your body and brain recharge.

🧼

Wash your hands

Wash with soap and water, especially before eating and after using the restroom.

🧠

Check in with yourself

Take breaks, connect with someone you trust, and ask for support when needed.

Why Health Made Simple is different

Unlike a general chatbot such as ChatGPT, Health Made Simple is built specifically for health education. Rather than relying on a quick, broad response, we aim to dig deeper into each topic and explain it using information from trusted health sources. Unlike Google, which can show many separate pages for people to sort through, our website compiles helpful information in one place and explains medical terms, conditions, and diagnoses in simpler language. Our goal is to make learning about health easier—not to replace a healthcare professional. Please check sources and ask a professional about personal medical concerns.
